# Third-party startup logo sources

- `grok_logo.png` is the unmodified Grok Image Compression GitHub
  organization avatar downloaded from
  <https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/16945593?s=200&v=4>. It is used
  in private Vulkanstorm builds to identify the selected Grok JPEG 2000
  decoder. Obtain explicit permission before public distribution.
- `soloud_logo.png` is the unmodified `soloud.png` supplied by the official
  SoLoud source repository at <https://github.com/jarikomppa/soloud>. SoLoud
  is distributed under the zlib/libpng license.

The logos identify bundled third-party components and do not imply endorsement
of Vulkanstorm by their respective projects.
